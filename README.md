# Mega-Man-Battle-Network-2---Full-Italian-Translation
Traduzione completa della rom per GBA menzionata
Hi everyone!

As a huge fan of the series, I’ve always found it a bit limiting that these titles were officially available only in English or Japanese. For this reason, I decided to roll up my sleeves: by using the amazing TextPet software and the support of AI to speed up the localization process, I managed to complete the Italian translation of the second chapter in record time!

I know many people have worked with similar tools in the past, but it’s still very hard to find complete Italian patches for chapters 2 through 6. My goal is to fill this gap and make the series more accessible to our community.

I’ve decided to share my work here with you. At the link below, you will find everything you need:

The translated ROM in GBA format.

Detailed instructions on how to extract and merge text (in case you want to contribute or customize them).

The technical "Prompt" I used to correctly instruct the AI to respect tags and GBA font limits.

I hope this work can be useful to anyone who wants to replay this classic in our language. Since it hasn't been fully playtested yet, if you find any typos or bugs, please let me know!

Enjoy!
